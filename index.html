🦈 SHARK EXPLORER

Dive into the world of sharks🌊

🦈 Great White

One of the most famous sharks in the world. Great white sharks are powerful predators found in oceans around the globe.

🔨 Hammerhead

Hammerhead sharks are easily recognized by their unusual hammer-shaped heads.

🐋 Whale Shark

The whale shark is the largest known shark species. Despite its enormous size, it mainly eats tiny organisms.

🐯 Tiger Shark

Tiger sharks get their name from the dark stripes that can appear on their bodies.

⚡ Mako Shark

Mako sharks are famous for their speed and are among the fastest sharks in the ocean.

👽 Goblin Shark

The goblin shark is a deep-sea shark with a very unusual appearance and a long, flattened snout.
